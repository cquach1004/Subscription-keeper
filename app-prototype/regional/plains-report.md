## Summary

- 23 providers, 69 plans: 38 with a price confirmed on the official site, 9 with a price from a third-party source, 22 with no published price.
- Third-party prices: Nuvera (CableTV.com, Aug 2026), Genesis Health Clubs ($79/mo, third-party site, Sep 2026), Club Car Wash (OmahaGuide, Aug 2025).
- Nextlink and Bluepeak list their regular price; intro discounts are in the plan notes.
- Viaero Wireless posts only "from" prices (plans from $25/mo, hotspot from $30), so its plans have no price.

## Notable gaps

- All four newspapers (Star Tribune, Post-Dispatch, Kansas City Star, Omaha World-Herald) block automated reading of their subscribe pages, so none has a price. Des Moines Register and Fargo Forum were blocked too and left out.
- Cox in Wichita now shows as "Spectrum (formerly Cox)", already in the app as Spectrum. Windstream Kinetic is already in the app from the Southeast scan; it also serves IA, MN, MO and NE.
- No new regional food delivery: Hy-Vee Plus is already in the app; Cub and Schnucks have no published membership price.
- Left out for lack of data: Black Hills Energy Service Guard, Xcel HomeSmart, Great Plains Communications, Gateway Region YMCA (St. Louis), Socket (MO), Hiawatha Broadband (MN).
- States may be incomplete for Club Car Wash (says about 10 states, 6 confirmed) and Plunkett's (ND and SD not confirmed).
