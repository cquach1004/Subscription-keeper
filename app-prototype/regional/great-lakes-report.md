## Things to know

- 24 providers and 62 plans. 16 plans are marked unconfirmed (third-party sources), and 21 have no price.
- Ten providers have no price at all: Surf Internet, i3 Broadband, cleveland.com, Detroit Free Press, Milwaukee Journal Sentinel, Wisconsin Athletic Club, FitWorks, the Appliance Service Plan, Rose Pest and Crew Carwash. Newspaper sign-up pages blocked the research.
- Hy-Vee Plus's $99 a year comes from 2020 launch coverage; the official page shows no current price. Metronet's page still carried June 2025 offer terms.
- Mediacom lists only the 9 states that could be confirmed; the company says about 22.
- Left out: Kroger Boost and TDS Telecom (near-national), XSport (now LA Fitness), Wil-Kil (now Terminix), Market Wagon (price and states not confirmed). No regional mobile carrier (UScellular was sold to T-Mobile in 2025). No child care or elder care providers found.
