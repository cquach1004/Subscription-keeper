## Notes

- 17 providers, 56 plans; 13 plan prices are not confirmed or not published (FPB NEXTBAND upper tiers, YMCA of Greater Louisville, all three newspapers, Vulcan's custom plan, and Glasgow EPB's dated rate notice).
- **Promo prices:** C Spire Fiber shows year-1 rates (later-year and regular prices are in the entry's notes). MaxxSouth gig prices are 3-year locked offers. Point Broadband prices are new-customer promos with an AutoPay discount.
- **C Spire Wireless:** its plan page shows two different price sets. We used the fine-print table (from $60 for one line).
- **Glasgow EPB:** prices come from its own rate notice from Nov 2024; nothing newer was found, so they're marked not confirmed.
- **Gaps:** no regional grocery, home, home security, child care, elder care, shopping or auto services found. Mississippi has only C Spire and MaxxSouth.
- **Left out:** Tidal Wave Auto Spa (332 sites in 30 states, close to national; also dropped from the Southeast batch). Tullahoma LightTUBe (prices only from a 2024 news story, no confirmed official site).
