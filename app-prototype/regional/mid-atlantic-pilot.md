## Left out on purpose

- Cox: Charter bought it (Aug 2026) and it is becoming Spectrum, which is already in the app as a national provider.
- ShopRite, Weis, Wegmans: no delivery membership of their own (Wegmans uses Instacart+, already in the app).
- Sport&Health now redirects to Onelife Fitness; Club Fitness has no Mid-Atlantic clubs.

## Weak spots

- Gyms don't post prices online, so all gym prices are blank.
- Giant Food and Stop & Shop sites were down; Giant's price is from 2023 and Stop & Shop's is blank.
- Several newspaper prices are blank or from older snapshots because the pages load prices dynamically.
- Some service-area lists (Optimum, Brightspeed, Retro Fitness, Albertsons FreshPass) come from partial or third-party coverage pages.
