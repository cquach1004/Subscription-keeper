## Notes

- 21 providers; 9 plans not confirmed on the provider's own page; 18 plans have no price.
- Salt Lake Tribune dropped its paywall in May 2026; its $10/mo membership price comes from Nieman Lab, not the Tribune.
- Ziply's $20 Fiber 100 may include a promo; Visionary Broadband prices are for Casper only; Deseret News and Town Pump prices come from their outside checkout vendors.
- Salt Lake County Rec: the rate sheet's columns were unclear, so prices are left blank (possible values in the notes). Union Wireless unlimited prices may cover more than one line.
- Nothing found for lawn and pest, home security, child care, elder care or shopping. Wyoming (3), Nevada (3) and Montana (4) are thin compared with Colorado (9) and Utah (7).
- Skipped: Moxie Pest, TDS and Movement gyms (too close to national), Cox in Nevada (becoming Spectrum, already in the app), Quantum Fiber (likely becoming AT&T Fiber, unconfirmed), and Rise Broadband, Blackfoot, Nemont and Strata (no prices found).
