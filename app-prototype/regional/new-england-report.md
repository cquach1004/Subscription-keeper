## Notes

- 19 providers, 46 plans; 17 plan prices are not confirmed or not published.
- Food is thin: Hannaford, Wegmans, Big Y, Price Chopper/Market 32 and Roche Bros use Instacart, Shipt or DoorDash instead of their own pass. Shaw's is Albertsons, already in the app.
- Boston Globe price left blank: its help page says $6.93/week (about $30/mo) but a 2025 news report says about $36.
- Fidium prices are first-year promos; Edge Fitness prices are from one club and vary by location ($15.99 to $49.99/mo).
- Skipped: Cox and Frontier (large multi-region carriers), Gannett papers and Boston Herald (no reliable prices). No regional child care subscriptions found.
