## Notes

- 18 providers: 12 in Arizona, 6 in New Mexico. New Mexico is thinner; no pest, home-service or child-care provider there had a usable listing.
- 8 plans have prices from comparison or review sites (Wyyerd 1 Gig, all 4 Plateau plans, 2 Defined Fitness plans, Crooked Sky Farms CSA).
- No price published: newspapers (Albuquerque Journal lists $9.99 and $15.99 without saying per month or year), car wash clubs (priced per location), Burns Pest ("starts at $399", no frequency), Sunrise Preschools, Village Health Clubs, some Wyyerd and Kit Carson tiers.
- Kit Carson Internet: the official grid says $50/mo, a Feb 2026 press release says from $30; we used $50.
- CSA farm shares are priced weekly; the monthly figure is weekly x 52 / 12.
- Four providers also serve states outside the region (Super Star Car Wash, Goettl, Burns Pest, Plateau); those states are listed so they show there too.
- Gaps: no regional mobile carrier (Choice Wireless ended consumer plans Dec 31, 2024), no home security, elder care or shopping. Skipped as near-national: Cox, Truly Nolen, Western Exterminator.
