## Notes

- 23 providers; 12 plans not confirmed on the provider's own page; 26 plans have no price.
- Nothing found for shopping, mobile, home maintenance, home security, child care or elder care. H-E-B and Brookshire's have no paid delivery membership.
- Big-city newspapers (Houston Chronicle, Dallas Morning News, The Oklahoman, The Advocate, Tulsa World) don't show prices to automated checks, so those are blank.
- Johnson's Backyard Garden prices boxes by the week; the monthly figure is the weekly price times 52 / 12. New CSA sign-ups were on a waitlist.
- 24 Hour Fitness and Bulwark serve many states outside this region; they'll show only where listed.
- Skipped: Cox (now becoming Spectrum, already in the app), Vexus Fiber (now T-Mobile Fiber), Mister Car Wash (no full state list or prices), Farmhouse Delivery (no subscription).
