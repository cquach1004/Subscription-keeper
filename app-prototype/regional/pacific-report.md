## Notes

- 23 providers; 10 have a price that is missing or not confirmed: Farm Fresh To You, Honolulu Star-Advertiser, Anchorage Daily News, San Francisco Chronicle, In-Shape Health Clubs, Bay Club, The Alaska Club, Bouldering Project, Street Shine Hawaii Unlimited, Clark Pest Control.

- Seattle Times monthly figure is worked out from the official $6.93 a week.
- Portland General Electric's Green Future Choice is charged per kWh (about $0.0094), roughly $5 a month for an average home, so it has no fixed monthly price.
- Bouldering Project posts $94 to $135 a month plus activation but doesn't say which gym charges what, so no single price is shown.
- Gaps: LA Times, Sacramento Bee, Oregonian, San Diego Union-Tribune and OC Register price pages could not be read, so they are left out. No regional child care, elder care, home security or shopping subscriptions with published terms were found.
